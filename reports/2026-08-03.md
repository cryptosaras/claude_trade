### What is measured

**Measurement A — live-entry integrity.** For each of `whale_absorb`'s live
entries, locate the signal bar (the closed 15m bar whose close precedes the entry
by ~15 min, per the +15.0–15.7 min offset established Aug 2) and count how many
of the 15 constituent **1m** candles exist in the DB **today**. Report per entry:
candle count, and whether the entry's clauses still evaluate true on today's data.

*What this can and cannot show, stated up front:* today's DB is backfilled, so a
**transient** gap that has since been filled is invisible and stays unverifiable
(`candles` has PK `(symbol, tf, ts)` and no insert timestamp — Aug 2 Finding 4).
Measurement A therefore bounds the **permanent-gap** class only, which is a floor
on the hazard, not its size. A bar that is complete-now *and* wide-now — the UNI
Jul 27 signature — remains **consistent with** backfill and **not proof of** it.

**Measurement B — the enrichment test.** This is the sharper one, and it is the
reason the hazard is testable at all. If a gap manufactures the "no price
progress" clause, then **signal bars should be incomplete more often than random
bars**. Over a pinned window, on group symbols, compare:

- the fraction of **all** closed 15m bars assembled from <15 1m candles, against
- the fraction of **`whale_absorb` signal bars** assembled from <15 1m candles.

A large ratio is direct evidence the artifact manufactures signals. A ratio near
1 says the permanent-gap class does not, and leaves the transient hypothesis
where Aug 2 left it: unverified.

**Window, pinned now: closed 15m bars with close in
[2026-07-06T00:00Z, 2026-08-02T21:00Z].** Same start as the Aug 2 census so the
two are comparable; end pinned to a closed past minute. Group symbols for
Measurement B; all 10 live entries for Measurement A regardless of group.

All queries filter `tf='1m'` (standing check). Both measurements run **twice**
and require byte-identical output.
