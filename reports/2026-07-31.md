The pre-registered pooled prediction was 32.5% actual against ~35.5% required at
R:R 1.87. **Measured: 32.0% actual, R:R 1.90, required 34.5%.** The prediction
essentially reproduced, so - as pre-registered - the per-strategy table below is
read as a *decomposition*, not a discovery.

**Net terms** (what the strategy actually kept), n >= 20 only:

| strategy | n | win% | R:R | required win% | gap | net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vwap_revert_short` | 304 | 33.9 | 2.05 | 32.8 | **+1.1** | +1,041 |
| `range_fader` | 108 | 27.8 | 2.02 | 33.1 | -5.3 | -1,770 |
| `ema_trend_rider` | 42 | 19.0 | 1.83 | 35.3 | -16.3 | -1,528 |
| `sweep_reclaim` | 35 | 37.1 | 1.32 | 43.1 | -6.0 | -555 |
| `pdhl_sweep` | 27 | 44.4 | 1.07 | 48.3 | -3.9 | -227 |

*(`whale_absorb` n=8 and `breakout_momentum` n=8 are below the floor and carry no
claim, per the pre-registration.)*

Every well-powered strategy sits within ~6 points of its own break-even line
except `ema_trend_rider`. **The book is not badly broken; it is marginally
broken.** That already refutes "stop placement is the problem" - a 5-point
win-rate gap is not a stop-distance artifact.

**Gross terms** (before fees and funding; slippage is inside the fill price, so it
is already charged here) is where it becomes unambiguous:

| strategy | n | gross win% | gross R:R | gross required | **gross PnL** | net | cost/trade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vwap_revert_short` | 304 | 33.9 | 2.56 | 28.1 | **+5,402** | +1,041 | 14.35 |
| `range_fader` | 108 | 27.8 | 2.60 | 27.8 | **-11.02** | -1,770 | 16.29 |
| `ema_trend_rider` | 42 | 19.0 | 2.64 | 27.5 | -806.87 | -1,528 | 17.16 |
| `sweep_reclaim` | 35 | 37.1 | 1.70 | 37.0 | **+5.18** | -555 | 16.00 |
| `pdhl_sweep` | 27 | 44.4 | 1.32 | 43.1 | **+74.67** | -227 | 11.19 |

**`range_fader`'s gross win rate is 27.8% against a gross break-even of 27.8%.
`sweep_reclaim`'s is 37.1% against 37.0%.** Three strategies land on the zero-edge
line to within a rounding error, over 108, 35 and 27 trades. Their entire net
loss is the fee bill: **-1,759 / -560 / -302 respectively.** Cost is a remarkably
stable **0.098-0.100% of notional per trade** across every strategy.

**This is the mechanism behind the closed dead end.** With zero gross edge, the
(win rate, R:R) pair is free to move along a constant-expectancy curve - which is
exactly what the net table shows: R:R 2.05 <-> 33.9%, 1.32 <-> 37.1%, 1.07 <->
44.4%, each pair almost exactly satisfying `p = 1/(1+R)`. Stop-distance tuning
slides a strategy **along** that curve. It cannot lift it off. Jul 28 and Jul 29
closed that lever empirically; this says *why*.

### The power calculation - the finding that displaces everything above

The stderrs are large. Required sample to distinguish "covers its costs" from
"no edge" at t = 2, computed as `n >= (2*sigma / hurdle)^2`:

| strategy | n today | sigma per trade | hurdle | **n needed** | t vs break-even |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vwap_revert_short` | 304 | 159.7 | 14.35 | **496** | +0.37 |
| `range_fader` | 108 | 143.8 | 16.29 | **312** | -1.18 |
| `sweep_reclaim` | 35 | 130.5 | 16.00 | **266** | -0.72 |
| `pdhl_sweep` | 27 | 108.9 | 11.19 | **379** | -0.40 |
| `whale_absorb` | 8 | 57.2 | 3.82 | **897** | *(n<20, no claim)* |
| `ema_trend_rider` | 42 | 93.8 | 17.16 | 120 | **-2.51** |

Scale-free form: since cost is a near-constant **0.10% of notional**, required n ~
`(2 * sigma% / 0.10%)^2`, where sigma% is per-trade PnL volatility as a share of
notional (1.1-1.5% across this book - `whale_absorb` is the most volatile at
1.47%, which is why its requirement is the harshest despite its small size).

**Consequences, stated plainly:**

1. **`n >= 20` in the validation gate is 13-45x below the sample any of these
   strategies needs.** At n=20, sigma/sqrt(20) ~ 30 USDT/trade against a ~15 USDT
   hurdle: **the noise is twice the signal.** A PF-1.15-at-n-20 promotion and a
   PF-0.9-at-n-30 retirement are both coin flips.
2. **`ema_trend_rider` is the only strategy in the live record with n >= 20 that
   is statistically distinguishable from break-even — and it is distinguishable
   in the wrong direction** (t = -2.51, p ~ 0.017 two-sided).
   **Correction to an earlier draft of this line:** it said n=42 "exceeds its own
   requirement of 120", which is arithmetic nonsense — 42 < 120. The n-needed
   column is calibrated to detect an effect of size *+hurdle*; detecting a
   **large negative** effect needs fewer trades. Its observed effect is
   -19.21 - 17.16 = **-36.4/trade, 2.1x the hurdle**, so the requirement is
   `(2 x 93.8 / 36.4)^2 ~ 27` trades, and it has 42. **The t-statistic is what
   carries this claim, not the n-needed column.** The conclusion stands; the
   reasoning as first written did not.
   Note the scope limit: this compares only the **five strategies with n >= 20
   live trades**. Nine strategies in the book have no live record at all and were
   retired on backtests not examined this session, and `whale_absorb` /
   `breakout_momentum` (n=8) were explicitly not judged. So this is **not** a
   claim about everything the lab has ever retired.
3. **`vwap_revert_short` at n=304 is still 190 trades short of its own
   requirement.** "The only ever net-positive strategy" and "statistically
   indistinguishable from break-even" are both true of it simultaneously.

**Limitations, and they matter:**

- **Trades are not independent.** Jul 21 documented repeat-entry cascades
  (TRB x5, JASMY x4, SYN x3). Correlated trades reduce effective n, so every
  "n needed" above is if anything **understated** - the bias runs against the
  comfortable conclusion, not toward it.
- **Versions are pooled.** `vwap_revert_short`'s 304 trades span v1, v2 and v3,
  which are materially different strategies. Its aggregate is a portfolio of
  three configurations, not one measurement.
- **PnL is fat-tailed**, so a t-statistic at n=27-42 is indicative, not exact.
- **Concentration (standing check):** `vwap_revert_short`'s gross is concentrated
  - the top 6 symbols supply **+4,655 of +5,402 (86%) from 86 of 304 trades**
  (NEAR +1,047, ORDI +935, TLM +759, SOL +704, CRV +649, ARB +561). Spread across
  six names rather than one, so this is **not** `pump_fade`'s singleton-symbol
  failure mode - but 86% of the edge from 28% of the trades is a real caveat on
  the only surviving edge claim in the book.
- **This is arithmetic on realized live trades.** It is not causal and there is no
  counterfactual: it says what the recorded distribution requires, not what any
  change would produce.
- **Checked and cleared: is gross ~ 0 a definitional artifact?** If `gross_pnl`
  were reconstructed from the *planned* SL/TP levels, and TP/SL were symmetric
  multiples of the same ATR, then "gross lands on the zero-edge line" would be
  partly built in rather than measured. **It is not.** `gross_pnl` equals
  `(exit_price - entry_price) x qty` **to the cent** on every row checked, i.e.
  it comes from actual fills. Stop exits land **0.030%** past the planned SL on
  every row — exactly the 0.0003 slippage constant, confirming slippage is inside
  the fill price as `account.py` documents. The loss side is therefore
  near-deterministic given entry, but the **win rate and the win side both remain
  free**, so the zero-edge result is empirical.
