### Item 4 — the 12h time-stop: this session's actual work, census first

`config/settings.yaml` → `max_hold_hours: 12`. Three sessions have called this
"the unexamined global" and nobody has measured it. The cheap version comes
first, on data already in the DB.

**Census:** closed `positions` where `mode='live'`, grouped **by strategy ×
exit_reason** — n, share of that strategy's trades, and net. **Per strategy, not
pooled**: holding periods differ ~3× across the book, so a pooled exit mix is
Simpson-confounded by strategy weighting.

**Stated limitation, up front, so it cannot be over-read later:** the net PnL of
time-stop exits is **not** a causal measure of the time-stop's value. It records
what happened at the 12h mark, not what would have happened without the cap. The
census can only establish whether the lever touches a **material fraction** of
trades. Sign is not evidence of direction.

**Branches, all fixed now:**

1. **T1 — immaterial.** If time-stop exits are <15% of closed live trades
   book-wide AND no single strategy has ≥20 of them → **item 4 closes on
   arithmetic.** The 12h global is not a material lever for this book. No
   backtest is run.
2. **T2 — material.** If any single strategy has **≥20** time-stop exits → run
   the backtest measurement below on that strategy.
3. **T3 — material but positive.** ≥20 time-stop exits whose net is positive →
   still run the measurement (branch T2), because a positive realized net at 12h
   says nothing about the counterfactual. Explicitly **not** grounds to skip.

**The backtest measurement (only if T2/T3 fires):**

- Target = the strategy with the largest time-stop exposure from the census.
- **Window B (Jul 13–27), pinned, `--end 2026-07-27T00:00`, run twice, identical
  n/PF/net required.**
- Exactly **two variants, both directions**: `max_hold_hours` **12 → 6** and
  **12 → 24**, plus the 12 baseline. Three configs, twinned. **No sweep** —
  a search over 4/8/18/36 would be selection on window B.
- **Single-strategy runs** (`--strategy NAME`), specifically to shrink the
  slot-reshuffle confound. That confound is expected to be **larger here than
  for any parameter tested so far**: hold time directly determines how long a
  position occupies one of the 6 slots, so changing it changes which *other*
  entries get taken. Report **n alongside PF** for every config; a large n shift
  means the comparison is partly reshuffle, not effect.
- Patch applied **in-process from a container-local driver**, never into
  `strategies/` or `config/` (both bind-mounted; the live engine hot-reloads).

**Pre-committed regardless of outcome: NO config change and NO status change.**
`max_hold_hours` is engine-wide — it silently re-times every strategy including
the one live experiment. The deliverable is a **measurement handed to the human**,
not an edit. This is fixed now so a favourable number cannot turn into "well,
but it's clearly better".
