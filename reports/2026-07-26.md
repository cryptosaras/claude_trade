### Where the "can't print low" belief came from — an arithmetic artifact

The Jul 23 report derived "backtest ~1.4 over Jul 16–21 while live bled 0.50"
by **subtracting** two runs (days-7 minus days-2) rather than running that window
directly. Run it directly and it is **0.62**. The subtraction was invalid:
PF is a ratio, nets don't decompose across differently-slotted runs, and the
two runs had different position-slot dynamics. That single arithmetic step is
the origin of the "the backtester has no discriminating power / effectively
can't print low" belief that shaped Jul 23, Jul 24, and my own first headline
today. **It was never true.** Do not subtract backtest windows; run the window.
