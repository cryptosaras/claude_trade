## Finding 1 — the backtestable span is 50 days, not 6 months

Measured per table on the VPS DB:

| table | starts | notes |
|---|---|---|
| candles `tf='1h'` | 2026-01-06 | ~180d deep, 99 symbols pre-April |
| candles `tf='1m'` | **2026-06-05 10:46** | 181 symbols — the real limit |
| funding | 2026-06-06 | aligns with the 1m start |
| regime | 2026-01-15 | hourly Jan–Jun, ~minute-granular in July |
| open_interest | 2026-07-05 | 197 symbols, ~10min cadence |

Consequence for future sessions: **do not plan a long out-of-sample test.** With
~50 days of 1m data, of which Jun 25 → Jul 26 is already consumed by tuning, the
uncontaminated remainder is ~16 days. That is enough to *refute* an existing
parameter set, and **not** enough to *validate a new strategy* to the gate's
standard (held-out profitability at n ≥ 20 plus 5-day incubation). That is a
data-availability fact, not a policy preference — and it is the honest reason
not to author a new strategy off a sweep right now.

## Finding 2 — funding lookahead is immaterial (retires the Jul 16 item)

`funding_asof()` fills pre-collection periods with the newest rate ≤ `end`,
which is technically lookahead. Magnitude, over all 524 real closed trades:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| avg hold | **1.50h** (median **0.54h**) |
| avg abs funding paid | **$0.196** |
| avg fees | **$15.12** |
| funding as share of fees | **1.3%** |

Median hold 0.54h against 8h settlement boundaries means most positions never
cross a settlement at all. Even taking the p90 observed rate (0.000214) as an
adverse constant, the drag stays ≈4% of fee cost. The bias is second-order and
the item is closed. (Fees/slippage untouched at 0.05%/0.03% — honesty rule.)

## Finding 3 — regime backfill is causal (no lookahead in Jan–Jun labels)

`backfill_regime()` (`app/collector/main.py:132`) computes ema/adx/slope with
trailing `.iloc[i]` windows and stamps each row at **bar close** (`ts + 1h`),
matching live `detect()` in `app/common/regime.py` exactly. Verified by reading
both. The historical regime labels are safe to split results by. One fidelity
note: Jan–Jun regime is hourly (720 rows/mo) vs ~5371 in July, so regime-gated
strategies see fewer transitions out-of-sample. Not disqualifying; state it.
