### Item 1 — the zero-trade BEAR window, and a retraction I am making before I look

**Retracted, on the record: yesterday's "5.9 trades per eligible day" rate and
its "`whale_absorb` clears n=20 around Aug 4" forecast.** Fact 3 is why. That
rate was computed by pooling all eligible hours and dividing — but the eligible
hours and the trades both came from essentially **one BEAR episode**. A rate with
no cross-episode replication is a burst, not a rate. The Jul 31→Aug 1 episode is
the **first independent BEAR replication attempt**, and its rate is 0.00/h
against the first episode's 0.249/h. Two episodes, incompatible rates.

**I will not compute a Poisson p-value on this.** Yesterday's own limitations
section documents repeat-entry cascades (TRB×5, JASMY×4, SYN×3), and ORDI appears
twice in these 8 trades. Arrivals are bursty, so a p-value from an
independent-arrival model would be a number that looks like evidence and is not.
The claim is qualitative and stops there: **the rate did not replicate.**

Zero has two candidate causes with very different consequences, and they are what
the session tests:

- **(a) The tape offered no qualifying setup.** `whale_absorb` needs, on the same
  15m bar: volume ≥2.5× the prior 20-bar average, range ≤0.9 ATR, bar low within
  0.3 ATR of the 12h low, 6h return ≤ −1.5%, and a resulting stop distance inside
  [0.5%, 5%]. A narrow conjunction, plausibly absent for 14 hours.
- **(b) Setups existed and the live engine did not act.** Data gap, silent
  exception, or eligibility logic. That is a **bug**, and a much bigger finding.

**Test, pinned and twinned:** backtest `whale_absorb` over the window and count
entries with `entry_ts ≥ 2026-07-31T15:03`. Command form
`--strategy whale_absorb --days 1 --end 2026-08-01T05:11`, **run twice, identical
n required**, void on non-empty `.err`.

**Plus a clause-level decomposition**, which the backtest alone cannot give: over
the 74 group symbols across that window, count how many closed 15m bars passed

and the full conjunction. This says *which* condition bound, not merely that

### Item 2 — the new-strategy design constraint, made arithmetic, and one candidate

Watchlist item 1 has now been deferred **three consecutive sessions**. The book is
16 strategies of which **15 are retired or paused**; `whale_absorb` alone is live
and it produced nothing for 14 eligible hours. Measuring the book again is not
what this session is short of.

Yesterday's constraint was stated in words: a candidate must produce a per-trade
edge **large relative to the 0.10%-of-notional cost**, roughly ≥0.3% gross per
trade, so that it is visible at n~50 rather than n~500. Step one is to make that
a **(win rate, reward:risk) requirement** rather than a slogan:

- Measure, over the whole live record, the average **win and loss size as a share
  of notional**. **Predicted: loss ≈ 0.45–0.50% of notional** (the stop-outs run
  ≈ −67 USDT on ≈ 14,000 notional).
- Then solve `p·W − (1−p)·L ≥ 0.30%` for the (p, R) pairs that clear it.
- **Pre-committed prediction of the conclusion: the constraint is reachable
  through reward:risk, not through win rate.** At R:R 2 it needs a win rate
  around 53% — versus 28–34% observed anywhere in this book — while at R:R 5 it
  needs only ~27%. If the arithmetic says otherwise, that is the finding.

This is not in tension with yesterday's "stop distance is a disconnected lever".
That finding says moving a stop on a **zero-gross-edge** strategy slides it along
a constant-expectancy curve. It does not say R:R is irrelevant to a strategy that
has real edge — it says R:R cannot manufacture edge where there is none. So the
candidate must target a move it has an actual reason to expect, and hold for it.

**Deliverable: one new candidate strategy, built and put through the full
validation gate** — tuning window, then a held-out window it was not tuned on,
twinned and pinned throughout.

**Pre-committed accept/reject, fixed now:**
- **Committed as `incubating`** only if held-out **PF ≥ 1.1** on twinned pinned
  runs with **n ≥ 20** in the held-out window, *and* measured gross edge per trade
  **≥ 0.20% of notional** (below the 0.30% design target but meaningfully above
  the 0.10% cost line — I am pre-stating this softer bar rather than moving it
  later to fit a result).
- **Otherwise not committed at all**, and the session reports a documented
