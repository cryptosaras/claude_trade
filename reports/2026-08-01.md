## Finding 2 — the design constraint, as arithmetic

Measured over all **532 closed live trades** (avg notional 14,956):

| quantity | measured |
|---|---|
| avg win | **+1.9985% of notional** |
| avg loss | **−0.7572% of notional** |
| gross win rate | **31.95%** |
| gross edge / trade | **+0.1234%** |
| fees + funding / trade | **0.1007%** |
| realized gross R:R | **2.64** |

**My pre-registered prediction of the loss size (0.45–0.50%) was wrong — it is
0.757%.** I had generalised `whale_absorb`'s ≈−67 USDT stop-outs to the book.

Solving `p·W − (1−p)·L ≥ 0.30%` at `L = 0.7572%`:

| reward:risk | avg win needed | **win rate required** | book's actual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.0 | 1.51% | **46.5%** | 31.95% |
| 2.64 *(book's actual)* | 2.00% | **38.4%** | 31.95% |
| 5.0 | 3.79% | **23.3%** | 31.95% |
| 8.0 | 6.06% | **15.5%** | 31.95% |

**The pre-committed conclusion holds directionally — reward:risk is the reachable
lever, not win rate — but my numbers were off** (I predicted ~53% at R:R 2 and
~27% at R:R 5; measured 46.5% and 23.3%), because they inherited the wrong loss
size.

The sharp version: **at R:R 5 the requirement is 8.6 points BELOW the win rate the
book already achieves.** Holding 31.95% while stretching to R:R 5 would yield
`0.3195×3.786 − 0.6805×0.757 = +0.70%` gross per trade — more than double the
0.30% design target and 7× the current 0.10% cost line. **The entire question is
whether win rate survives the target moving out**, and that is a measurement, not
an argument.

**Why this is not the closed stop-distance dead end.** Friction is a **fixed ~0.16%
of notional** (0.10% fees+funding, plus 0.03%/side slippage already inside the
fill price) and does **not** scale with how wide the trade is. So widening scales
the signal against a constant cost: scaling a **zero** gross edge yields zero —
which is exactly why `range_fader` (gross −0.10/trade) and `sweep_reclaim`
(+0.15/trade) could never be rescued by moving stops — while scaling a
**positive** gross edge lifts it clear of the hurdle. Yesterday's finding closed
the former case; this is the latter, and it needs a signal with measured positive
raw edge.

## Finding 3 — the candidate, and its gate

There is exactly one signal in this repo with a measured positive raw hold:
`whale_absorb`'s absorption event. Its own study reports the **raw 8h hold at
+1.02% net/trade (PF 3.09, n=175)** against the deployed strategy's **+0.31%**.
The exit is discarding two thirds of the studied edge.

[`strategies/whale_absorb_runner.py`](strategies/whale_absorb_runner.py) changes
**one variable**: `tp_r` 1.5 → **6.0**, expressing "do not clip" (a 6R move inside
8h is rare, so the 8h time exit dominates). Entry logic is **byte-identical** to
`whale_absorb` — verified programmatically, not by eye — and every entry param,
including `sl_atr_buf`, is untouched.

**The value was pre-declared from the study before either window ran**, so there
is no tuning window: **both windows are confirmation runs.** Committed as
`status: retired` so it takes no live slots while being measured (`--strategy`
overrides that for backtests).

**Contamination, stated rather than glossed:** the entry conjunction inherits
`whale_absorb`'s in-sample history — its params came from a study ending
2026-07-06, and Jul 13–27 was already spent as its gate's out-of-sample window.
So **both windows are held-out for the exit change, and neither is clean for the
entry.** This is weaker than a true two-window gate and is not presented as one.

### The result: REJECTED. The hypothesis is falsified, cleanly.

Both windows, both strategies, `--days 14`, pinned `--end`, all eight `.err`
empty, and **every one of the four runs twinned byte-identically** on n, win rate,
PF, net, gross and gross-per-trade.
`whale_absorb` is run on the identical windows as a **control**, so the difference
is attributable to the one changed variable:

| window | strategy | n | win% | PF | gross PF | **gross % of notional** | avg hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B** Jul 4 → Jul 18 | `whale_absorb` (tp_r 1.5) | 32 | **50.0** | **1.13** | 1.29 | **+0.197%** | 5.55h |
| **B** Jul 4 → Jul 18 | `whale_absorb_runner` (tp_r 6.0) | 30 | 43.3 | **0.64** | 0.73 | **−0.215%** | 6.51h |
| **A** Jul 18 → Aug 1 | `whale_absorb` (tp_r 1.5) | 22 | 31.8 | **0.37** | 0.43 | **−0.548%** | 4.72h |
| **A** Jul 18 → Aug 1 | `whale_absorb_runner` (tp_r 6.0) | 21 | 33.3 | **0.28** | 0.33 | **−0.625%** | 5.31h |

*(notional recovered as `fees_per_trade / 0.001`; the fee rate is exactly
0.001000 of notional, verified against all 532 live trades.)*

**The candidate is worse than the control on both windows, on every measure.** It
fails its pre-registered bar (held-out PF ≥ 1.1 and gross ≥ +0.20% of notional)
by a wide margin — PF 0.64 and 0.28, gross −0.215% and −0.625%. **It stays
`retired` and is not promoted.** No tuning, no second attempt at a different
`tp_r`: the value was pre-declared and the answer is the answer.

**Why it failed is the useful part, and it inverts my reasoning.** On window B the
TP clip was being **hit half the time** — `whale_absorb`'s win rate is **50.0%**
there. Moving the target from 1.5R to 6R cut that to 43.3% and pushed average hold
from 5.55h to 6.51h: winners that used to be banked at 1.5R now ran past their
peak and gave the gains back to the 8h time exit. **The bounce does not run to 6R;
it reverses.** The 1.5R clip was not leaving edge on the table — it was
*capturing* the edge at roughly the right place.

**The load-bearing premise was wrong, and it was the study's number, not the
arithmetic.** The cost argument in Finding 2 is unaffected — friction really is
fixed at ~0.16% of notional and really does not scale with trade width. What
failed is the claim that the absorption event has a **+1.02% raw 8h hold** to
scale up. `whale_absorb_runner` *is* approximately that raw hold (time-exit
dominant), and it produced **negative gross on both windows**. The 2026-07-06
event study's headline number **does not reproduce on either window that
post-dates it.** That is a finding about the study, and it should temper how much
weight this repo puts on the rest of that document.

**Reported honestly, the control is also failing.** `whale_absorb` itself is
**PF 0.37 / gross −0.548% on the most recent 14 days** (n=22) against PF 1.13 /
+0.197% on the prior 14 (n=32). **No action is taken on that** — per Jul 31's
power finding, n=22 is far inside the noise band (its own requirement is ~897
trades), and the live gate still reads n=8. It is recorded for the next session,
not acted on.
